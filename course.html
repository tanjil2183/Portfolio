Course Overview

This course connects personal growth with everyday academic, social, and professional situations. Learn at your own pace through the lesson video, reading material, and practice questions.

Learning Outcomes

  • Build confidence and a growth-oriented mindset.
  • Communicate respectfully and act professionally.
  • Apply human values and empathy in daily decisions.
  • Strengthen learning, presentation, time, and career skills.

How to Learn

  1. Open a lesson and watch its embedded video.
  2. Read the PDF in the page or open it separately.
  3. Complete the five-question practice quiz.
  4. Use the explanations to review before moving on.
  5. When ready, attempt the final assessment.

The final assessment contains 30 questions from the 12 course lessons. To complete the course and receive your certificate, you must score at least 18 out of 30.
